WFC and research on older PCs

WordFinance intends to use a portion of the funds it receives from WFC sales to support open-source graphics research. The goal is to investigate what it would take to run GTA VI on older PCs.

Official WFC page See the experiments

How sales could fund the research

Resources WordFinance receives from WFC sales could pay for the work below. The share of proceeds, schedule and transfers to the project have yet to be published.

Test on older hardware

Repeat the same experiments on Radeon RX 570 and RX 580 cards and compare results across drivers.

Develop rendering techniques

Test ways to reduce GPU memory use and render geometry on cards without mesh shaders.

Publish the evidence

Make source code, images, timings and reproduction instructions public so others can check the results.

WFC purchases are optional and are separate from donations. The code, reports and community are freely accessible. WFC is operated by an independent partner. The official page provides current token information and availability. Cryptoassets can lose value.

What we have measured

The lab has rendered its own scene at 720p on a physical Radeon RX 570 and published frame times and image comparisons for Direct3D 11 and Vulkan.

GTA VI compatibility on this PC remains unverified. Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The research has no affiliation with Rockstar Games.
